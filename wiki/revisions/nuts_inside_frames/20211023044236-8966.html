Category:Anti-patterns

Introduction

Nuts can be threaded onto bolts while inside of hollow frame members if needed in some niche cases.

Challenges

Accessing nuts with wrenches after project is assembled.

Approaches

Use the next longer size bolt and a washers on the reverse side to allow a nut to tighten fully.
