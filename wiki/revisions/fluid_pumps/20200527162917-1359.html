Current page History
Problem

Moving large volumes of liquid is tedious by bucket or barrel. Pump and hose require less effort and are more convenient.

Rationale

Manufacured for RVs, boats, and other off-grid systems, this style of pump is relatively inexpensive and widely available. Wherever possible, Replimat uses self-priming, particulate tolerant, membrane pumps manufactured with pressure switches and for a variety of DC voltages, for RVs.
