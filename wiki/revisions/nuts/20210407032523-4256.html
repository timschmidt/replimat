Introduction

Nuts secure bolts through holes in frames and grids.

Challenges

Nuts come in a variety of shapes, sizes, features, and configurations.

Approaches

Nylock nuts have been selected to reduce the tendency of frames to loosen with vibration.

Life cycle

Stripped, rounded, or abraded nuts degrade to abrasive pellets for ball mills.
