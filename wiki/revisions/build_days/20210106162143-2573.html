Current page History

Build days are fun! And offer a number of benefits and opportunities to individuals and the community. They enable rapid and collaborative problem solving, assembly of complex projects with great speed, and educational opportunities for all involved.

Challenges

  • Communicating enough about how to build with Replimat in both distilled and time-release forms
  • Holding a safe space for experimentation and mistakes
  • Getting everyone's hands and minds on.

Approaches

  • Projectors cycling through inspiring build photos
  • Short presentation of Replimat construction fundamentals
  • Lots of parts
  • Lots of tools
  • A goal, competition, or build inspiration
