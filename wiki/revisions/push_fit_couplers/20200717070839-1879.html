Challenges

There are a dizzying variety of hoses, pipes, couplers, fittings, adapters, and other plumbing fixtures. How can we reduce complexity while retaining broad compatibility.

Approaches

Wherever possible, Replimat prefers to use Push fit compression fittings for liquid and air pumpinp, and to adapt existing fittings to this system.
