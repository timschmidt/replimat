Problem

Power transmission often requires rotating shafts with sprockets, brakes, pulleys, gears, wheels, and many other types of devices attached in unique configurations.

Approach

A round steel shaft with a https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Key (engineering) keyway allows for similarly keyed interchangeable components to be positioned securely along it's entire length. Such a shaft can be manfactured from round stock with as little as a https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Broaching (metalworking) broach and a hammer.
