Replimat techniques name clever ways to combine parts to solve common challenges of designing and assembling projects.

Techniques are the verbs of the Replimat language. "Techniques" is currently overloaded to include things which might be called "assemblies" as well as pure verbs, if this becomes troublesome "assemblies" can be instantiated.
