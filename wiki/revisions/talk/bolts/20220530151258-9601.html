38.1mm frame (from 8020 Inc.)

This frame is pre-punched with 21/64 inch holes which are intended for 5/16 inch hardware. Equivalent metric hardware is listed below:

1 Bolt: 5/16 inch x 2 inch ~ M8x60\\

2 Bolt: 5/16 inch x 4 inch ~ M8x100\\

3 Bolt: 5/16 inch x 6 inch ~ M8x140\\
